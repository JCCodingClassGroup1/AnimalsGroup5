Incredible Animals

This website highlights some of the most unique animals in the world. The extremes of the natural world.

Speed

Peregrin Falcon

Clocking in at an amazing 389 km/hr the Peregrin Falcon is the fastest animal on Earth.

Bannana Slug

At a truely glacial 10 m/hr the bannana slug is the slowest moving animal on Earth.

Size

Blue Whale

Weighing in at a whopping 250 tonnes and with a maximum recorded size of 110 ft the Blue Whale is the largest and heaviest animal on Earth.

Brazilean Flea Toad

So tiny it can fit on your finger nail the smallest animal is the Brazilean Flea Toad.

Lifespan

Immortal Jellyfish

With a potentially unlimited lifespan the immortal Jellyfish is has the longest lifespan of any animal.

Strength

African Bush Elephant

Able to lift an incredible 6,000 kg the African Bush Elephant is the animal capable of lifting the most weight.
